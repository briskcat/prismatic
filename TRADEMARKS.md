# Names

"Prism FX" and "Prismatic" are the names of this project and its maker. They
aren't covered by the code license in `LICENSE`.

You're free to build, modify, share and even sell the code under the terms of
the AGPLv3, which means sharing your source too. But if you distribute a
modified version, please give it its own name, and don't use "Prism FX" or
"Prismatic" in a way that suggests it's the original or an official release.
Saying it's "based on Prism FX" is fine.
