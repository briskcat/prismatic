Prism FX, by Prismatic
======================

A VST3 effect for Windows (64-bit), built from the CHOMPI effects and tape
looper.

Install
-------
1. Before unzipping, right-click the downloaded zip, choose Properties, tick
   "Unblock" at the bottom, and click OK. (Windows marks downloaded files, and
   some DAWs won't load plug-ins that still carry that mark.)

2. Unzip it, and copy the "Prism FX.vst3" folder to:
      C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
   Windows will ask for permission to copy there.

3. Restart your DAW, or rescan plug-ins.
   - Ableton Live: Options > Preferences > Plug-Ins, turn on "Use VST3 Plug-In
     System Folders", then click Rescan.
   - Others (Reaper, FL Studio, Bitwig, Cubase...): rescan VST3 plug-ins.

Prism FX shows up under the manufacturer "Prismatic".
